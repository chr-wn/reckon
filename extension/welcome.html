Reckon for Chrome

Make a prediction from any page without leaving it.

  1. Press … (or click the toolbar icon). Type the question, ↵ to the probability, ⌘↵ to post. The link is copied so you can paste it anywhere.
  2. ⌥Y ⌥L ⌥M ⌥W switch between yes/no, how long, how much and when. ⌥/ lists every shortcut.
  3. Google Calendar: open an event and press F (or click the Reckon row). You get “Will I finish <event> within <its length>?”, due when the block ends; just type your probability.
